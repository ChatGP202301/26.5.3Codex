OEM & ODM Solutions for Water Purifier & Filter Cartridges

Express Water provides OEM and ODM solutions for carbon block filters, GAC filters, T33 filters, PP filters, water purifiers, and water dispenser systems.

Carbon Block Filters

Activated carbon removes chlorine, VOCs, unpleasant odors, and tastes from water with stable performance and durability.

GAC Filters

Granular activated carbon filters eliminate impurities and pollutants by adsorption for multiple purification applications.

T33 Filters

Inline post-carbon filters provide final polishing to enhance taste, clarity, odor, and color.

PP Filters

Melt-blown polypropylene filters provide sediment, sand, rust, and particulate filtration to protect downstream components.
