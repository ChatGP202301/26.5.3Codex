Product Introduction

The UDF granular activated carbon filter is designed for drinking water systems and RO pre-treatment. It helps reduce chlorine, organic chemicals, unpleasant taste, and odor before the water reaches later filtration stages.

Technical Features

Activated carbon and coconut shell carbon options are available. The connection can be flat end cap, female thread, MD plug-in type, or silicone ring seal according to different system designs.

Applications

Used in residential RO systems, commercial water purifiers, under-sink systems, dispenser systems, and OEM filter replacement packs.
