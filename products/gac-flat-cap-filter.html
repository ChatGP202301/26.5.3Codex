Product Introduction

The GAC Flat Cap Filter uses granular activated carbon to adsorb chlorine, tastes, odors, and organic compounds. The flat cap structure is simple to install and suitable for common water purifier systems.

Technical Features

The filter supports stable flow, reliable adsorption, and easy cartridge replacement. It can be configured for different housings and private-label programs.

Applications

Suitable for drinking water purifiers, RO pre-treatment, dispenser filtration, household systems, and light commercial applications.
