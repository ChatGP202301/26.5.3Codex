Product Introduction

The PP Spun Filter is melt blown with 100% pure polypropylene microfibers. It removes rust, silt, scale, sediment, dirt, coarse sand, and sand from water.

Technical Features

High filtration efficiency, low pressure drop, high dirt holding capacity, wide chemical compatibility, and excellent thermal stability for demanding filtration conditions.

Applications

Water treatment, chemical industry, pharmaceuticals, food and beverage, HVAC, and industrial pre-filtration.
