Product Introduction

Big Blue UDF Filter cartridges are designed for larger housings and higher flow applications. They provide carbon adsorption for chlorine, odor, taste, and organic chemical reduction.

Technical Features

Large cartridge volume increases contact time and dirt holding capacity. The cartridge can be configured for whole-house and commercial filtration requirements.

Applications

Whole-house water filtration, commercial pre-treatment, industrial water systems, beverage systems, and large RO system pre-treatment.
