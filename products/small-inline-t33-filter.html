Product Introduction

The Small Inline T33 Filter is commonly used as a final polishing stage after reverse osmosis. It improves water taste, reduces residual odor, and can support mineralized water filter cartridge configurations.

Technical Features

Compact inline design, convenient installation, optional mineralized media, stable taste improvement, and compatibility with residential and commercial RO systems.

Applications

RO water purifiers, drinking water systems, refrigerators, coffee equipment, and dispenser filtration.
