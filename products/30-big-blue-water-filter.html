Filtration Stages

The PP Filter removes large particles such as rust and sediment. The RO Membrane removes contaminants such as heavy metals, bacteria, and viruses. The CTO Carbon Block Filter removes remaining contaminants and impurities, including chlorine and other chemicals.

Applications

Residential drinking and cooking water, commercial and industrial purified water, manufacturing, laboratory, and high-capacity whole-house filtration needs.
